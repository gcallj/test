# Strategy v5 trades analyst revisions, frozen before its first run

After v4, the owner chose to test analyst revisions next: free, new data that
no earlier strategy used.

## Data

Börsvärlden publishes each broker's rating and target-price change in Swedish,
with a publication timestamp. Example: "SEB höjer riktkursen för Volvo till 300
kronor (280) och upprepar köp".

`analyst_se.py` reads the site's public sitemap and keeps the broker articles
that name a Large Cap company. The site rate-limits with HTTP 429, so only the
articles the test uses are fetched. `analyst_parse.py` splits each article
into clauses and attributes every action to the company named in the clause:
upgrade or downgrade, initiation, and new versus old target. Commissioned
research ("motiverat värde", paid by the company) and magazine tips are kept
apart, and no hypothesis uses them.

## Hypotheses

Three hypotheses come from published work. Their thresholds, horizons and
pass rule are fixed in `data/strategy_se_v5.json` before any revision is
joined to a return:

- **R1, upgrades and buy initiations, predict a rise over 20 sessions.**
  Womack 1996; Irvine 2003.
- **R2, downgrades, predict a fall over 60 sessions.** Womack 1996. An ISK
  cannot sell short, so R2 is only used to stop buying a downgraded name.
- **R3, more target raises than cuts on a day, predicts a rise over 20
  sessions.** Brav & Lehavy 2003.

## Test

The protocol is v4's (ADR 0008):
- trade at the first open after the publication day;
- control: same 63-day-return quintile;
- weekly series, with Newey-West t ≥ 2 and a stationary bootstrap;
- Holm correction across R1–R3;
- mean event return above the 0.70% round trip;
- criterion 1 for the portfolio of the families that pass.

## Considered Options

- **Mid and Small Cap insider purchases** have the strongest Swedish evidence,
  but need prices of delisted companies (paid data). Deferred by the owner.
- **Magazine tips and commissioned research** are left out. Tips mostly move
  prices on the day they are published, and commissioned research is paid for
  by the company it covers.

## Consequences

The prior is weak. In Germany, a one-day delay removes most of the gain from
a recommendation change. Börsvärlden's coverage of Large Caps is also thin
before 2021. A pass would justify paper trading, not real money; a failure
closes this source. The article cache lives under `output/analyst` and is
regenerated by `analyst_se.py`.
